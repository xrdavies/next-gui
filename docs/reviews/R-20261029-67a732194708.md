## Change and reference

Bars now occupy separate group slots or stack at matching X keys, with positive
and negative baselines and independent Y axes. Limits include bar edges and signed
totals. Rendering, interior hit tests, markers, image clipboard and saved images
use the same geometry. Source order stays intact; unsorted lookup indexes and
small block extrema trees avoid repeated full scans of dense duplicate keys.
Overflowing sums saturate to finite limits and groups beyond six retain positive
width. These are deliberate robust extensions of the
[original PlotDock](https://github.com/sqlitebrowser/sqlitebrowser/blob/v3.13.1/src/PlotDock.cpp).
Duplicate/near-key stacking follows the original bundled
[QCustomPlot source](https://github.com/sqlitebrowser/sqlitebrowser/blob/v3.13.1/libs/qcustomplot-source/qcustomplot.cpp)
and [documented signed/axis rules](https://www.qcustomplot.com/documentation/classQCPBars.html).
Exact QCustomPlot pixel spacing, borders, category rotation and all control states
are still outside this subset.

Stacked bars is a checked Plot action, with a backward-compatible default and
persistence in the existing JSON/native-project extension. View and selection
survive toggles; Fit recalculates the signed automatic limits. Category tick density
now counts visible labels instead of rounding a padded numeric interval.
