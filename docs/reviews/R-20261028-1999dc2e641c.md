Active sequence history is retained; disabling AI preserves PK membership and drops its obsolete sequence record through the rebuild.

Explicit AI conversion allows nullable source IDs to receive automatic IDs. Existing IDs are copied before NULLs to avoid allocation collisions; the SQLite engine rejects incompatible data or exhausted sequences and the enclosing savepoint restores the original table.
